# Retail operations glossary (customer document)

Terms the business uses in meetings and reports, and what they mean.

## Complaint
Anything a customer raises with the service team. Logged as a support ticket.

## Points
Loyalty points members earn on purchases and spend on rewards. Every earn and spend is a loyalty transaction.

## Parcel
A shipment on its way to a customer's delivery address.

## Money back
A refund paid to a customer after they return goods.
